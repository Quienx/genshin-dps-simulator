# Genshin DPS simulator: engine guide

A character is **one JSON file** in `_data/characters/` plus **one import line** in `_lib/characterData.ts` (`CHARACTER_FILES`). A general block engine runs it. Engine code only changes to add **generic** features, never for one particular character.

- Schema and validation (zod, at load): `_lib/characterSchema.ts`, `_lib/actionSchema.ts` and `_lib/engine/schema.ts`.
- Actions (talents, `actions`, `forms`): compiled in `_lib/actionSchema.ts` (`getCharacterActions`).
- Simulation: `_lib/engine/runtime.ts` (state, bonuses, triggers, hits) and `_lib/engine/kitAdapter.ts` (when each action starts and which form it takes).
- Shared systems: `_lib/engine/modules/*.ts`, which expand into ordinary blocks.

## Rules before proposing anything new

1. **A temporary state or mode is a `status` block.** There is no separate "state" type, and none should be created.
   - For Natlan characters, the mode is **Nightsoul's Blessing** from the `nightsoul` module (`nightsoulBlessing`): Mualani's surfing, Varesa's Fiery Passion, Mavuika's modes.
   - For characters outside Natlan (Flins, for example), the mode is their own `status` with a trigger that applies it.
2. **What changes inside a state is the talent's `forms`**, with `"while": { "status": "<id>" }`. They work for the Normal Attack (with its own combo), the Charged Attack, the E or the Q, including a cheaper Q like Kablam.
3. **Passives and constellations are `bonus` or `trigger` blocks** (`source: passive` or `constellation`). They can depend on the state (`while`, `if`), but they are not states.
4. **A mechanic several characters share goes in a `systems` module.** One that belongs to a single character goes in blocks in its JSON. Check the module table before creating a module.
5. **`hook` is the last resort**, only when no block can express the rule.
6. **An engine change must meet three conditions:** it is a generic feature, it has its test in `__tests__/engineFeatures.test.ts` (using `fixtures.ts`), and it leaves the golden unchanged (`scripts/sim-golden.ts --compare`) unless the change is intended.
7. **Reference data:** frames, hitmarks and kit rules come from gcsim (`internal/characters/<name>/*.go`, multipliers in `zz_<name>.dm.go`). If a rule is uncertain, mark it `partial` or `not-simulated` in `constellations` instead of guessing.

## Teams (`_lib/engine/simulateTeam.ts`)

A team is up to 4 characters on **one clock** (a shared event queue) against one enemy. Each member keeps its own runtime (blocks, resources, cooldowns, stats) and its own `DamageContext`; one member is on the field at a time.

- **Rotation:** team blocks name their member in the action id (`"iansan:burst"`, see `toTeamActionId`). A step of another member swaps to it first: the swap is pressed after the previous action's `swap` cancel (else its end) and 1 s after the last member came in, and takes `swapDelayFrames` (gcsim's `swap_delay`) until the new member is on the field and the swap cooldown starts. The engine's default is 0; the team screen uses 12 (`DEFAULT_SWAP_DELAY_FRAMES`, the usual gcsim config). Dodge and Wait stay with the member on the field (`isFieldAction`): the trace records them under that member, the editor shows them once in a General group, and a dropped or moved one is renamed to the member on the field there (`stampFieldActions`). A kit's special dash (Ayaka's) would be its own `move` action, in its member's group. **Swap** (`<member>:swap`, common action of kind `swap`) brings that member on the field and takes no time of its own, so a Wait or Dash after it is theirs; in the editor, a block dropped where its member is off the field gets one before it (`swapInBefore`), and Swaps left doing nothing are dropped (`dropIdleSwaps`). It is once in the General group: a dropped one goes to the next block's member (else the first one off the field, `aimSwap`), and the edit bar picks its target (`retargetSwap`). Alone, it is hidden. Teams only play their rotation (no automatic list).
- **Team buffs:** a `teamBuff` bonus applies to the other members' hits (`"active"`: only while the receiver is on the field), valued with the giver's stats and state at the hit (`getTeamEffects`). One that converts a stat (`ofStat`: Iansan's Scale from her ATK) reads the giver's stat at that moment: its own bonuses that hold and what the team gives it (resonance, Chevreuse's A4), but no `extra` bonus (gcsim's "Extra": the Scale and Grace of Kenosis), so buffs never feed each other.
- **Auras** (team mode): a Pyro, Electro or Cryo hit of U units (`gauge` on a row's timing, a form's or action's hits, or a `hit` op; 1 by default, 0 for none) on a bare enemy leaves 0.8 × U, decaying over (2.5 × U + 7) s. A hit that reacts with the aura (`REACTION_TABLE` in `simulateTeam.ts`) takes `uses` × U units off it: Pyro↔Electro → Overloaded (uses 1, a hit of its own dealt by the hitter), Pyro on Cryo → Melt ×2 (uses 2), Cryo on Pyro → Melt ×1.5 (uses 0.5). Melt multiplies the hit itself (it overrides a kit's `bonus.reaction` there) and fires `reaction:Melt` / `party:reaction:Melt`. One aura at a time; an element with no entry for the aura leaves it as it is. The solo screen's "Overloaded every … s" options are off in a team.
- **Elemental application ICD:** a row's timing, an action's or form's hits, or a `hit` op can set `icd` (gcsim's tag): a character's hits sharing it apply their element on the 1st, 4th, 7th… hit, or the first one 2.5 s after the count restarted. Without `icd`, every hit applies. Take the tags from gcsim (`ICDTag…` / `ICDGroup…`).
- **Unregistered characters:** a team member can carry `loaded` (from `loadCharacterFile()`), so a file can play in a team before it is in `CHARACTER_FILES` (the Melt tests use a Cryo copy of Mualani).
- **Party uses:** `use: "party:<key>"` fires when any member uses an action, by its id, group or kind (Varesa's Kablam is a `burst`): Iansan's C4.
- **Party triggers:** `party:<key>` fires on any member's hit (`party:reaction:Overloaded`, `party:elemental` for the Nightsoul Burst); `active:<key>` on the active member's. A `hit` op with `asAttacker` (and `element: "attacker"`) is dealt by the member whose hit fired the trigger, with its stats (Nicole's Arcane Projections).
- **Alone**, a character is always on the field and is its whole party, so a team of one deals exactly what the character deals alone (`__tests__/team.test.ts`).
- **UI:** the only page is `/genshin-dps-simulator` (`_components/timeline/`, entry point `CombatTimelinePage.tsx`); the preset rotation and the team's state are in `_lib/team.ts`. Varesa's solo team options (`isSoloTeamField`) are hidden in a team: the real teammates give those buffs.
- **Counted duration:** a run counts its `duration` (60 s) and nothing after. A rotation of one's own (edit mode, `runOwnRotation`) still plays every block, to the second after the last one ends (`duration`), but hits from `countedDuration` on are logged with `counted: false` and add nothing (`countUntilFrame` in the damage context); the chart dims that stretch. In edit mode the axis goes 10 s past the last block, to drop new ones there.
- **Particles:** a member's particles are shared when they are caught: the member on the field gets the full Energy, the others 60%; same element 3 Energy each, another element 1, clear ones (`particles` with `clear: true`, Favonius) 2; all × the receiver's ER.
- **Field:** triggers `when: { swap: "in" | "out" }` fire when the character comes on or leaves the field; the condition `{ onField: true }` holds while it is on it (always, alone). Lost Prayer keeps gaining stacks every 4 s of combat off the field and keeps them on swap, as seen in-game (gcsim resets them on swap: a deliberate difference).
- **Elemental resonance**, from the team's elements, as data (`RESONANCES`: a `bonus` with an optional `whileAura`): 2 Pyro, Fervent Flames (+25% ATK for everyone, `resonance/fervent-flames`); 2 Cryo, Shattering Ice (+15% CRIT Rate while the enemy has Cryo, `resonance/shattering-ice`); 2 Electro, High Voltage (no bonus: an Electro particle for the team on Overloaded, once every 5 s). The character screen does not include it, so solo runs never apply it.
- **Filling** (`fillWhileWaiting`, off by default): while the next member's action recharges, the member on the field plays its automatic list instead of swapping in to wait.
- Not modeled: swap-in effects (Nicole's 3 s on field), Chevreuse's C1 Energy for the active member, healing.

## JSON reference

### Character screen stats (`ascensionStat`, `screenStats`, `screenStat`)

The stat fields hold the character screen's totals. Instead, a build can enter its five artifacts (main stat and four substats each, `values.artifacts`); while that is on, `parseValues` replaces `atk`, `hp`, `def`, `em`, `cr`, `cd`, `energyRecharge` and `elementalBonus` with `computeScreenStats` (`_lib/artifacts.ts`): base stats × (1 + %) + flat, plus 5% CRIT Rate, 50% CRIT DMG and 100% ER, and:

- `ascensionStat: { stat, value }`: the level 90 ascension bonus (Project Amber's `specialProp`, `upgrade.promote` last entry). Required.
- `weapon.substat`, plus a weapon row's `screenStat: { stat, param }` for an always-on passive that shows on the screen (Angelos' ATK%, Surf's Up HP%), valued from `params[param]` by refinement.
- `artifactSet.screenStats: [{ stat, value }]`: a 2-piece that shows on the screen (Harmonic Whimsy ATK +18%, Celestial Gift ER +20%).

Stats are `SCREEN_STATS` (`_lib/characterSchema.ts`); `elementalDmg` is the character's element. Conditional bonuses stay blocks, as with typed totals, so nothing is counted twice.

### Talents (`talents`)

- **`rows`:** `multiplier` rows (`components` per level). Each row may have `timing: { action, hitmarks, end, cancels, element, windupSkippedAfterNormal, events, category, label }`.
  - Rows with `action: "normal"`, in order, make the combo; `"charged"` is the Charged Attack. In the E or Q, the rows are the cast's hits.
  - **Normal, Charged and Plunge hits are Physical by default** (rows, forms and extra actions alike): set `element` for catalysts. E and Q hits default to the character's element.
- **Talent level (E/Q):** `end`, `cancels`, `energyCost`, `cooldown: { frames, delayFrames, deferred, charges }`, `castDuringPrevious`, `note`.
- **`actions`:** extra actions (a hold E, plunges, `kind: "move"`). Their hits are the rows whose `timing.action` is their id, or explicit `hits`. They take `end` and `cancels` too.
- **`forms`:** `{ id, replaces?, while, element?, hits?, end?, cancels?, cooldown: "shared" | "none" | "waived" | {…}, cooldownId?, energyCost?, group?, label?, shortLabel? }`.
  - `replaces` defaults to the talent's action (`normal` in the Normal Attack talent).
  - The first form whose `while` holds **when the action starts** (after waiting for its cooldown) wins.
  - A Normal Attack form with its own rows has **its own combo**, and the combo position carries on from the previous action.
  - By default a form counts as the action it replaces (`group`), so it fires that action's `use` and `hit` triggers.
  - A form without its own `end` keeps the replaced action's animation: its `end`, `cancels` and `castDuringPrevious`. A `"shared"` cooldown is the replaced action's after `actionRules` are applied. A `"waived"` cooldown waits for the replaced action's cooldown like it but does not start it (Chevreuse's C4: after her Q, two Hold E shots off cooldown, while a status lasts).
- **`boostedBy`:** the constellation that raises the talent by 3 levels.
- **`end` and `cancels`:** `end` is the frame (from the action's start) at which any next action can start. `cancels: { "<next>": frame }` lets some next actions start earlier; the key is the next action's id, group or kind (`"plunge"`, `"dodge"`, `"skill"`), and the most specific match wins. Take both from gcsim's frame tables (`end` = the cancel into the slowest common action, usually Attack; `cancels` = the faster ones). A Charged Attack whose windup was skipped counts from its shorter start.

### Action rules (`actionRules`)

`{ action, requires?, message, cooldown?, replacedBy? }`. If `requires` does not hold, the step is skipped and shows `message`. For transformations, prefer `forms` over `replacedBy`.

### Blocks (`blocks`, plus those of `weapon` and `artifactSet`)

| Type       | Key fields                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `bonus`    | One of `stat` (+ one of `value`, `valueRow` or `valueField`), `infuse`, `reaction`, `setCategory` or `addMultiplier`. Optional: `ofStat` (atk, hp, def, em; any stat: 0.1 `atkPercent` of `hp` is 1% ATK per 1,000 HP), `capRow`, `teamBuff`, `perStack: { state, max }`, `cap`, `scaleBy: { field }`, `match: { actions, categories, elements }`, `while: { <id>: { gt, gte, lt, lte } }` (all conditions must hold), `ifFlag`.                                                                              |
| `resource` | `min`, `max`, `initial` (number or `{ field }`), `noteFormat`/`unit: "percent"`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `status`   | `durationFrames`, `endsWith`, `ticks` (`everyFrames` may be fractional, rounded up per tick; `firstAfterFrames`; `gauge`, `icd`, `icdGroup`; the ticks are hits of the block whose action applied the status), `stacks: { max, durationFrames, holder? }` (each stack lasts on its own; not combinable with the other timing fields; `holder: "active"` gives each stack to the member on the field when it is added, and a team buff reading it counts only the receiver's stacks: Chevreuse's C6), `drain`. |
| `trigger`  | `when`, `if`, `icdFrames`, `do: [ops]`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

`valueRow` / `capRow` read a talent row at the character's talent level (boosts included); `valueField` reads a number form field. A `teamBuff` bonus never applies to the character's own hits: it is a buff for the party, reported with `{buffValue:<id>}`.

`bonus` stats: `dmgBonus`, `critRate`, `critDamage`, `flatMultiplier`, `atkPercent`, `hpPercent`, `defPercent`, `flatAtk`, `flatHp`, `flatDef`, `elementalMastery`, `flatDamage`, `defReduction`, `defIgnore` and `resShred`. The stats the user enters are the character screen's totals, so bonuses are only the conditional ones.

### Triggers (`when`)

| Key               | Fires                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `hit`             | On a hit. The value can be an action id, a group, a row (`elementalSkill/skill-cleave`), a category (`category:Plunge`), an element (`element:Hydro`), any non-Physical hit (`elemental`) or any hit (`*`). `crit:<any of those>` fires only on a CRIT; `reaction:Overloaded` on a transformative reaction's hit (which fires no other key). |
| `use`             | When an action is used: its id, the form's id or the group.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `event`           | A named moment of the Charged Attack (`charged/absorb`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `resourceChanges` | When a resource changes; with `change: "increase" \| "decrease"`, only that way.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `heal`            | After a heal op of this trigger (its id), or of any (`*`): Chevreuse's C6 adds a stack on each heal of her E.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `statusEnds`      | When a status ends (removed, drained or expired), on its first inactive frame. A stack status ends when its last stack runs out or it is removed while a stack is up.                                                                                                                                                                        |

### Conditions

`{ resource, gt|gte|lt|lte }`, `{ status, atEvent? }`, `{ flag }`, `{ field, … }`, `{ ready: <action> }`, `{ previous: <id|group|kind> }` (only while planning), `{ all }`, `{ any }`, `{ not }` and `{ hook }`.

### Operations (`do`)

- `addResource` (with `amount`: a number, `{ ifStatus, then, else }`, `{ row }` or `{ field }`, and `budget`)
- `setResource`, `multiplyResource`
- `applyStatus` (with `afterFrames`), `removeStatus`, `addStack`
- `reduceCooldown` (cuts the charge that is recharging; the charges queued behind it move up too), `startCooldown` (an action id or a cooldown id)
- `hit` (`multiplier`, `row` or `multiplierField`, `element`, `category`)
- `reactionHit` (`"Overloaded"`): a transformative reaction's own hit, with the character's EM; only EM and RES shred bonuses that match it count (its category is the reaction's name)
- `particles` (a number or `{ min, max, maxChance }`)
- `heal: { effect, target?: "active" | "party", from? }`: a heal. HP is not simulated, but it is logged (the inspector shows who heals, from what, what it does and whom) and fires `heal` triggers. `from` (`skill`, `burst`, `constellation`…) when the block's own source is not specific enough
- `count` (`by: "gained"` adds what the previous `addResource` gained), `note`, `warn`, `hook`
- `addResource` with a `budget` spends from it only what was actually gained (after the cap).

All accept `ifFlag`, `unlessFlag`, `if`, `icdFrames` and `delayFrames`. A `removeStatus` with `delayFrames` only ends the instance that was active when it was scheduled (or, if none was, the one already scheduled by an `applyStatus` with `afterFrames`).

### Rotation, warnings and results

- **`automatic`:** a priority list `{ use, if?, ifFlag?, unlessFlag? }`; the first entry that holds is used. Entries are checked at the earliest frame any of them could start (a cancel), so an entry turned down never delays one that can start sooner; the chosen one then starts at its own earliest frame. Without it, the default is: E when ready, otherwise Normal.
- **`rotation.extraActions`:** blocks for the editor palette, such as sequences (`steps`) or actions with `performed`.
- **`rotation.palette`:** optional list of action ids (talent actions or `extraActions`) the editor palette shows, in that order. Every other action is hidden from the palette but still works (an automatic copy may use it). An unknown id fails at load.
- **`actionWarnings`:** `{ action, unless, message }`.
- **`details`:** templates such as `{uses:skill}`, `{hits:Normal:Pyro}`, `{count:x}`, `{resource:id}`, `{cooldownCut:skill}`, `{bonusHits:id}`, `{bonusStacks:id}`, `{buffValue:id}` (a team buff's value, every stack, from the screen totals; 0 while its flag is off) or `{uptime:status}` (frames; for a stack status, while at least one stack is up), with the formats `|number`, `|fixed1`, `|seconds` and `|percent` (of the run) and `a/b` ratios.
- **`fields` and `defaults`:** form fields. `checkbox` fields are the flags; `number` fields are the values `field` and `initial` read.

## Gotchas

- `match.actions` compares against the id of the action **performed**. For a bonus to apply to a form (for example `fieryNormal`), include its id.
- A stack added on a frame starts counting on the next frame (the activity log shows it that way too).
- Every resource and status id a file names is checked at load (`_lib/engine/validateReferences.ts`), and an E/Q with hits needs an `end`.
- A custom-rotation Q waits for particles already flying (`particles` with `afterFrames`); the automatic list only uses the Energy there is.
- Known gap: an `automatic` entry's `if` is checked before waiting for its cooldown, so it can fire after its condition ended (`it.fails` test in `engineFeatures.test.ts`). Fixing it needs planning lookahead without running events.
- A Charged Attack form runs as a plain cast (it loses `events` and the windup).
- Reactions: amplifying ones (Vaporize and Melt) and the transformative Overloaded, in `_data/reactions.json`. Solo runs have no aura model: a kit makes Overloaded with a `hit` trigger (`element:Electro` or `element:Pyro`) whose ICD sets how often it happens (the `overloadedEvery` field, 1-5 s), and Melt / Vaporize with a flagged `bonus.reaction`. Teams use the auras above.
- CRIT triggers in expected-damage runs fire on every hit, with `particles` and `count` scaled by the chance. With an ICD, the chances of the hits within one cooldown window (from the first hit that could fire it) add up to 1 − ∏(1 − CRIT Rate), as if the cooldown started with the first hit.
- A status with `endsWith` is never applied while its parent is down.
- Team buffs: a support reports them (`teamBuff`, `{buffValue}`, `{uptime}`); Varesa receives them as flagged bonuses whose values are form fields, in windows that start at her first hit and repeat with each support's cooldown. Nothing links the two simulations: copy the support's numbers into Varesa's fields.

## Adding or checking a character

1. Write the JSON with gcsim's frames and rules and the multipliers from the talent tables.
2. While writing it, validate and simulate it without registering it, using `loadCharacterFile()` (`_lib/characterData.ts`) and `simulateLoadedCharacter()` (`_lib/engine/simulateKit.ts`).
3. Add the import line to `CHARACTER_FILES`. The invariants in `__tests__/invariants.test.ts` then cover it automatically.
4. Add cases to `__tests__/goldenCases.ts` and regenerate `scripts/sim-golden.json` (`npx tsx scripts/sim-golden.ts`) **only** when a change in results is intended.
